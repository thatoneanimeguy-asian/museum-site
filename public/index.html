Blacksburg Museum and Cultural Foundation

F I Y

We are the stewards of two museums near Blacksburg - The Alexander Black House and St. Luke & Odd Fellows Hall

Our mission is to preserve, interpret, and promote Blacksburg's art, history, and cultural heritage.

The Alexander Black House

Many features, such as rotating exhibitions, programs and events, children's room, gift shop, library and genealogy center, Alexander Black bedroom display, outdo or exhibitions, education and workshop room, staff offices, and much more!

St. Luke & Odd Fellows Hall

The last remaining New Town building, which was a neighborhood that grew amid a racially divided climate. Its residents didn't have the same resources as Blacksburg's white population, so they built a stable community in response to create meaningful lives for themselves.
