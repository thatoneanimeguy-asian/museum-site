About

Learn about the foundation’s mission and stewardship of local museums.

The Blacksburg Museum and Cultural Foundation preserves the region’s stories through museum operations, exhibitions, education, and community engagement. We care for the Alexander Black House and St. Luke & Odd Fellows Hall, sharing local history and cultural heritage with visitors and learners.
